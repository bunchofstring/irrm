# Buy and hold

## Extra state fields
None.

## Extra position fields
None. It defines no stop levels, so it places no resting stop order.

## BUY rule
Buy at the asking price (a paper BUY) when the strategy has no open position.

## SELL rule
Hold until further notice.

## End-of-day liquidation
Does NOT apply. Positions may be held overnight.

## Log line additions
None.
